Straight questions

Straight answers.

Do you really publish your prices?+

Yes — the full book is right on this page, the same one our techs carry. The $49 diagnostic is credited in full toward any work. The number we say before starting is the number on the invoice.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?+

Yes. A person — not a recording — answers any hour. Average response inside the Loop: 47 minutes. Call (713) 555-0190.

What does a 200A panel upgrade cost in Houston?+

From the book: $2,100–$3,400 with the city permit and inspection included. The range depends on the home's age and the service entrance. The exact price is confirmed on site, in writing, before we start.

Do you install EV chargers?+

Yes — Level 2 (Tesla, ChargePoint, Emporia and others), $480–$1,400 installed, almost always in one visit. We include the load calculation to confirm your panel can take it.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Texas state license TECL #21940, insured and bonded. A master electrician signs off on every job, and we pull the city permit ourselves whenever one is required.

¿Hablan español?+
