SEC. 06House rules

How we work. No exceptions.

Thirty-four years of doing this boils down to eight rules. Every tech signs them before they ever drive a truck.

i.

We answer the phone

A person, within three rings, around the clock. If you call at 3am, we talk at 3am.

ii.

The price is the price

In writing before we start. If it takes twice as long, you pay the same number.

iii.

A master signs everything

No job closes without a master electrician's sign-off. Not one.

iv.

Late? The diagnostic is free

We give you a two-hour window and we hit it, or the $49 is on us.

v.

Fix first, sell never

We tell you what's urgent, what can wait, and what you don't need. In that order.

vi.

We always pull the permit

If the job needs one, we file it ourselves. Unpermitted work is a time bomb at resale.

vii.

Your home like we found it

Shoe covers, drop cloths, shop-vac. Drywall cuts patched and painted.

viii.

Lifetime workmanship warranty

Our labor, guaranteed for as long as you own the home. In writing.

SEC. 09Straight questions

Straight answers.

Do you really publish your prices?+

Yes — the full book is right on this page, the same one our techs carry. The $49 diagnostic is credited in full toward any work. The number we say before starting is the number on the invoice.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?+

Yes. A person — not a recording — answers any hour. Average response inside the Loop: 47 minutes. Call (713) 555-0190.

What does a 200A panel upgrade cost in Houston?+

From the book: $2,100–$3,400 with the city permit and inspection included. The range depends on the home's age and the service entrance. The exact price is confirmed on site, in writing, before we start.

Do you install EV chargers?+

Yes — Level 2 (Tesla, ChargePoint, Emporia and others), $480–$1,400 installed, almost always in one visit. We include the load calculation to confirm your panel can take it.

Are you licensed and insured?+

Texas state license TECL #21940, insured and bonded. A master electrician signs off on every job, and we pull the city permit ourselves whenever one is required.

¿Hablan español?+
